on this device, the server only fetches the streams; your browser puts them together. what it can't do, the server does.

how it works

The link goes to this site's server, which uses yt-dlp to find the video and fetch it from the site, since a browser cannot read another site's pages. On this device, each stream passes through to your browser as it arrives, and joining the picture to its sound, or converting to mp3, happens here; the finished file never exists on the server. On the server, or when this device cannot finish (a stream cut into pieces, a very large file, a browser without the codecs), the server makes the file and keeps it for an hour at most.

YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky, SoundCloud, Twitch and many other sites work. Videos that need an account, live streams, videos over three hours and files over 4 GB do not. Save only what you have the right to keep.
