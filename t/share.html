    transfers

      can't see the other device?

      On the same Wi-Fi, devices with share open appear by themselves, and a device that moves to another network finds the devices there. A VPN, Private Relay, or IPv6 on only one of them can hide them from each other; then open the link, or scan the code, on the other device. The link works from any network. It stops working when this page closes, when you take a new link, or half a minute after a connection drops.

      The receiver chooses what to accept. Keep both tabs open until the transfer finishes. Accepted files go to your downloads as they arrive, the way any download does, so a transfer can be as large as your disk. Safari, and a browser that holds the downloads back, keep them in this browser's storage until you download them; where there is none, as in some private windows, they are held in memory, up to 256 MB at a time. Remove received files to free space.

      Files travel over an encrypted WebRTC connection and stay out of this site's server storage. The server only introduces the two devices and passes the connection details. A relay may carry encrypted data when configured.
